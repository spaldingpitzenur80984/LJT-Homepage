I am a first-year Ph.D. candidate in the HKUST NLP Group at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), pursuing a Ph.D. in Computer Science (2024-Present) under the supervision of Prof. Junxian He. I graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024 with a B.Eng. degree (2020-2024), where I received the Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship and was advised by Prof. Junxian He during my undergraduate studies.

My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning. Specifically, my research interests include:

- LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
- Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)
- LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability
